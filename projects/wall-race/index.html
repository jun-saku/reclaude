Two players take turns on this phone. Works offline.

How to play
  • Be the first to get your pawn to the far side of the board (the tinted row).
  • On your turn, either move one square (tap a dot), or place a wall.
  • Walls are two squares long and block everyone. You get 10 walls with 2 players, 7 with 3, 5 with 4.
  • To place one, switch to Wall, tap or drag on the board, tap again (or Turn) to switch direction, then Place.
  • A wall may never shut a player off from their goal completely.
  • Facing another pawn, you can jump over it. If a wall or pawn is behind it, step diagonally instead.
