How to play
  • Each square shows a coloured number; every one appears twice. Corners are free for everyone.
  • On your turn, tap a card in your hand, then one of its squares to place a chip. You draw a new card.
  • Wild cards go on any empty square. Take cards remove one opponent chip (not from a finished line).
  • A card with both squares taken is dead: tap it and swap it for a new one.
  • Five chips in a row, column or diagonal is a line. Two lines win with 2 players or teams; one with 3 players.
