Word Grid

Swipe across touching letters to make words. You have 3 minutes per board.

How to play

  • Drag through letters that touch, including diagonally. Lift your finger to play the word.
  • Words need 3 or more letters, and each letter can be used once per word. Qu counts as two letters.
  • Points: 3–4 letters 1, 5 letters 2, 6 letters 3, 7 letters 5, 8 or more 11.
  • The daily board is the same for everyone and changes at midnight UTC. You get one go.
