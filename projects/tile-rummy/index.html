How to play
  • 106 tiles: 1–13 in four colours, twice each, plus two jokers (☺). Everyone starts with 14.
  • A run is 3 or more of one colour in a row (4-5-6). A group is 3 or 4 of one number in different colours.
  • Jokers stand in for any tile.
  • Your first play must be new sets from your own rack worth at least 30 points.
  • After that, rearrange the table any way you like, as long as every set is valid when you tap Done.
  • Drag a tile onto a set, or onto the green table for a new set. Or tap tiles to pick them up, then tap ＋ on a set or New set.
  • Drag tiles around your rack to put them in your own order.
  • Sets you changed get a green outline when they're valid and a dashed red one when they aren't. Tiles from your rack this turn are tinted.
  • Everyone sees the table change live while the player on turn arranges it.
  • Can't or don't want to play? Draw a tile and your turn ends. First to empty their rack wins.
  • When the pool runs out and everyone passes, the fewest tiles wins.
