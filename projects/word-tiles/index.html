How to play
  • Tap a tile on your rack, then tap a square to put it there. Tap a placed tile to take it back. 🔍 zooms the board.
  • Each turn, place tiles in one line (across or down) with no gaps. The first word must cover the ★ in the middle; after that, every word must touch the tiles already there.
  • Every word you make, including the ones crossing your line, must be a real word. You score them all.
  • Light and dark blue squares triple or double a letter; pink and red ones double or triple the whole word. Using all 7 tiles in one turn earns 50 extra points.
  • The blank tile can be any letter and scores 0. Instead of playing, you can swap tiles (while 7 or more are in the bag) or pass.
  • The game ends when someone uses their last tile with the bag empty, or after 6 turns in a row without points. Tiles left on your rack count against you, and go to whoever went out.
