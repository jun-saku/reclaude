How to play
  • A murder just happened in the house. Everyone is a resident with 2 secret items as their alibi. One of you is secretly also the killer.
  • 3 suspect weapons are shown to all; only the killer knows which one is real. There's also one 🎟️ Theatre ticket (the alibi item).
  • On your turn, move to any room, then either search it (look through its pile in secret: take a card, drop one, trade one for one, or just look) or call a vote (from round 3).
  • You hold at most 2 cards. Hand and pile sizes are public.
  • After each round an event hits: Search (everyone in a room draws blind), Blackout (draw blind from any room), Lock, Unlock (a closed room opens) or Police sweep. Anyone pushed to 3 cards shows a random one and drops one.
  • Votes: if at least half agree, the accused shows their hand. The ticket saves them; exactly their 2 secret items clears them; anything else convicts. If they survive, the accuser misses a turn. A convicted innocent becomes a 👻 ghost who can move one card blind between rooms each round.
  • The killer wins by starting a turn in an unlocked room holding the real weapon plus one of their 2 secret items or the ticket, with the other two in that room's pile.
  • Everyone else wins by convicting the killer, or in the final vote after round 10.
