How to play
  • On your turn, tap an empty cell or one of your own to add an orb.
  • A cell bursts when it holds as many orbs as it has neighbours: 2 in a corner, 3 on an edge, 4 in the middle.
  • A burst throws one orb into each neighbour and makes those cells yours, which can set off more bursts.
  • Once everyone has played, a player with no orbs left is out. The last colour on the board wins.
