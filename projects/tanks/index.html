How to play
  • Enemy tanks roll in from the top. Keep them away from the eagle at the bottom: if it's hit, the game is over.
  • Drive with the pad (or arrow keys / WASD) and tap Fire (or Space). One shell at a time.
  • Shells break bricks; steel and water stop you. You can hide under trees.
  • Your team shares its spare lives. Clearing a wave earns one more.
  • Grey tanks are steady, light ones fast, bronze ones shoot fast shells, and dark armoured ones take four hits.
  • Online, the game carries on if someone leaves; they can rejoin with the code.
